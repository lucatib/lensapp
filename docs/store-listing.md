## Long description

LensApp is a camera built for looking closely at colour. Zoom in on the detail, switch on the torch, and read which RAL Classic shade it is.

ZOOM
Get close to small details without moving. Pinch, drag anywhere on the screen, double-tap, or use the vertical slider. This is real camera zoom, not a crop of the image.

TORCH
Turn the torch on to light a sample in a dark corner, a cabinet or a workshop.

MEASURE RAL
The colour under the centre reticle is read live and matched to the nearest RAL Classic colour, several times a second. You get:
• RAL code and name, with the ΔE (how close the match is)
• Three runner-up matches
• The measured colour as RGB, hex and Lab

MORE TOOLS
• White reference: point at a white or grey card in the same light and tap "Set white ref" to remove the colour cast of the lighting
• Hold: freeze the frame and put the phone down; the reading stays on screen
• Save: store the frame in your gallery with the reticle and the full reading on it
• Copy: put the reading on the clipboard

HOW IT WORKS
LensApp averages a small patch of the image, converts it to CIE Lab and compares it with the RAL Classic table using CIEDE2000, the standard formula for perceptual colour difference.

GOOD TO KNOW
• A phone camera is not a spectrophotometer. Results depend on lighting and the surface. Treat the result as a close guide, not a certified measurement.
• Neutral greys (RAL 7xxx, 9006, 9007) are the hardest to tell apart, because they differ mainly in lightness.

PRIVACY
